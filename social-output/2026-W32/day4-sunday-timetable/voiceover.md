# Voice-over script — sunday-timetable

Target length: 30–45 seconds (98 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Quick warning if you're planning a Sunday day trip from Florence to Siena. The Sunday timetable is a different animal. After the nine-ten bus from Florence, there is no 131R at all until ten past two in the afternoon — so realistically, a Sunday day trip means catching that nine-ten. Coming back, Sundays and holidays see only eight departures all day, and the last bus leaves Siena at ten past seven in the evening. On weekdays you'd have until quarter to nine. Checked against the official summer timetable on the third of August. Details at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
