# Voice-over script — airport-cheapest-route

Target length: 30–45 seconds (96 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Landing at Florence Airport and heading straight to Siena? You don't need a taxi. The cheapest reliable route costs eleven euros ninety, checked on the third of August. Here's how it works. Tram line T2 leaves from right outside arrivals and takes you into central Florence for two euros. From there, the 131R bus runs to Siena for nine euros ninety — as long as you buy at the machine, the counter, or in the app. Buy from the driver and that leg alone becomes sixteen euros. The full step-by-step route is at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
