# Voice-over script — bus-vs-train-arrival

Target length: 30–45 seconds (90 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Here's the thing nobody tells you about getting from Florence to Siena: the bus and the train don't arrive in the same place. The 131R bus drops you at Via Tozzi, beside Piazza Gramsci — right next to the old town. The railway station? It sits about two kilometres below the hill, so arriving by train means planning an uphill connection before your day trip even starts. If Piazza del Campo is the goal, that one fact decides the whole trip. The full bus-versus-train comparison is at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
