# Voice-over script — tourist-tax

Target length: 30–45 seconds (101 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Booking a night in Siena? Budget for the tourist tax, because it lands on top of the room price you see. The numbers, published by the Comune di Siena and checked on the twenty-third of July: two euros per person per night if you're in a B&B, an apartment or a short let, and one euro per person per night in a hostel or campsite. For a couple on a two-night stay in an apartment, that's eight euros at check-in. Small number, but it's the kind you'd rather know before you arrive. The full cost breakdown is at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
