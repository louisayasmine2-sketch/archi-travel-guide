# Caption — 75-minutes-the-131r-bus-runs-about-75

Bus or train from Florence to Siena? On the current summer timetable the 131R bus takes about 75 minutes. The train usually takes 1 hour 20 to 1 hour 45, depending on whether it runs direct or changes at Empoli.

The bus is a road service, so traffic can add time — build in a margin. And the clock is only half the decision: the two arrive in very different parts of Siena.
