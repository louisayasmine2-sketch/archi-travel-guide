# Voice-over script — 75-minutes-the-131r-bus-runs-about-75

Target length: 30–45 seconds (107 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Bus or train from Florence to Siena? On the current summer timetable, checked on the third of August, the one three one R bus takes about seventy-five minutes. The train usually takes longer — roughly one hour twenty to one hour forty-five, depending on whether it runs direct or changes at Empoli. The bus is a road service, so traffic can add time; build in a margin rather than planning to the minute. And the clock is only half the decision — the bus and the train arrive in very different parts of Siena. The full comparison, every number dated and checked, is at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
