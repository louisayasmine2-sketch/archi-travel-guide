# Voice-over script — eur1980-as-a-day-trip-by-bus

Target length: 30–45 seconds (101 words — aim for a measured, unhurried read).

Costing a day trip to Siena from Florence? By bus, return transport is nineteen euros eighty per person when you buy at the counter — thirty-nine euros sixty for two — as checked on the third of August. That is before attraction tickets, and it is the whole transport bill: a day trip pays no tourist tax and no room. The catch is timing. Some of Siena's best tickets are sold on the day only, in limited numbers, and they can be gone by the time a day-tripper arrives. The full costing, day trip against overnight, is at affittacameregliarchi dot com.
