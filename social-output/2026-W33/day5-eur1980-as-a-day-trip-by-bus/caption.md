Costing a Siena day trip from Florence? Return bus transport is €19.80 per person bought at the counter — €39.60 for two — before attraction tickets. No tourist tax, no room to pay for.

The catch day-trippers hit: some of Siena's best tickets are sold on the day only, in limited numbers, and they can be gone by the time you arrive.
