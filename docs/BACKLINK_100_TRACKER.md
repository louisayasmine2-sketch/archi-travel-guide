# 100 backlinks: the plan and the tracker

Owner-side execution document. The target is **100 earned links from ~100
distinct referring domains**. The rules live in
`docs/LINK_EARNING_WORKLIST.md` and are not restated here beyond the one that
matters: **we earn links, we never manufacture them** — a tactic that cannot
be described honestly in an outreach email does not get used.

## 1. The honest arithmetic

100 earned links is a 12–18 month programme, not a campaign. Anyone
promising it faster is selling manufactured links, which are the one thing
this site cannot afford. The maths that gets there:

| Channel | Realistic conversion | Effort | Target links |
|---|---|---|---|
| Journalist source platforms | 1 placement per ~10 answers | 3 answers/week | 15 |
| Resource-page and blogger outreach | 5–10% of well-matched pitches | 8–10 emails/week, personalised | 30 |
| Film-location / set-jetting niche (Bond page) | high fit, small pool | one-off round + news pegs | 8 |
| Institutional: study-abroad, language schools | slow, durable links | one-off round, revisit termly | 10 |
| Place directories done properly (Atlas Obscura et al.) | near-certain if submission is good | one-off effort each | 3 |
| Podcast guesting | 1 booking per ~5 pitches | 1 pitch/week until booked | 4 |
| Newsletters and link roundups | editors take good originals | notify on every major publish | 6 |
| Organic citations (people find and cite us unprompted) | grows with the above | keep publishing originals | 24 |
| **Total** | | | **100** |

The last row is not padding: it is the compounding return of the first
seven. The legacy B&B links arrived exactly this way. Track it in Search
Console (Links → Top linking sites, checked monthly); it lags by weeks.

**Weekly cadence that sums to the target** (roughly 4–5 hours):
3 journalist answers, 8–10 personalised outreach emails, 1 podcast or
editor pitch, and the monthly Search Console check. Stop any channel that
produces nothing for 8 weeks and move its time to one that does.

## 2. Prospect roster

Grouped by channel. Every name came from search on 25 August 2026 —
verify before pitching. Statuses live in the tracker table (§3); this list
is the pool it draws from.

### A. Journalist source platforms (target: 15)

Register as "editor of an independent, fact-checked Siena and Tuscany
guide; resident sources; every figure carries a visible check date".

- Source of Sources (SOS) — the free successor to HARO's volume
- Qwoted (free tier first) — fewer, higher-authority requests
- Featured.com — structured Q&A, low competition per question
- SourceBottle — long-running, has a travel category
- #journorequest on X/Bluesky — speed matters more than polish

Answer only requests where we hold a dated, sourced figure. The pitch is
the figure and its check date, never the site.

### B. Tuscany and Siena travel bloggers (target: 30, pool below is a start)

The pitch is always one specific page of theirs plus one checkable thing of
ours that improves it. Pool so far (worklist §2A names not repeated):

- Maybe Both — personal Tuscany guide with onward links

Finding more: search their post titles for "Siena" + a fact we hold

date, link optional. Log every send in §3.

### E. Place directories done properly (target: 3)

- Atlas Obscura — submit the bottini as a place entry: our own licensed
  photos, detailed description, practical visit facts. Priority goes to
  complete submissions with three-plus good photos. The entry itself
  carries community weight even before any link.
- Comparable curated-place sites surfaced while researching: pitch only
  where user submissions are editorially reviewed. Never mass-submit to
  directory farms — that is the manufactured-link line.

## 4. What Claude does versus what only you can do

Claude-side, on request: draft any pitch from the templates (worklist §3)
against a named target page; build new linkable originals (the Bond page
pattern: L'Eroica, deeper bottini material, Francigena sections); prepare
the Atlas Obscura submission text and photo shortlist from our licensed
set; refresh this roster with new research rounds.

Owner-side, irreducibly: sending every email from your own address,
registering on source platforms, podcast appearances, and pressing
"submit" anywhere that requires an account. Links a machine could place
are links Google is built to ignore — the human bottleneck is the moat.
