## The one rule, before anything else

**We earn links; we never manufacture them.** No bought links, no PBNs, no
comment or forum spam, no automated placement, no link exchanges, no
expired-domain tricks. This is not piety — it is arithmetic. The entire
strategy of this site is trust: verified facts with visible check dates,
honest disclosures, scanners that fail the build over a single hedge phrase.
An affiliate site lives on Google's trust, and one link-scheme penalty erases
every ranking this repo's discipline has earned. A tactic that cannot be
described honestly in an outreach email does not get used.

The corollary: outreach is a **human task**. Every email comes from a person,
says who we are, and offers something genuinely useful. Nothing in this file
is automatable, and that is by design.

**Wikipedia warning:** never add links to our own site to Wikipedia. It is

them; that is the only version that counts.

## 2. Target categories, with starting candidates

Candidates surfaced by search on 18 August 2026 — **verify each page exists,
links out, and is alive before pitching**. These are starting points for your
own list, not a complete map.

### A. Tuscany/Siena travel bloggers with resource or guide pages

The pitch: not "link to us" but a specific, checkable fact or tool of use to
*their* readers ("your Siena post mentions parking — our ZTL guide documents
the fine process with a July 2026 check date, if useful to your readers").

- Untold Italy (untolditaly.com) — Tuscany guide with onward links
- Mama Loves Italy (mamalovesitaly.com) — Siena family-travel angle
- Bon Traveler (bontraveler.com) — first-timer Siena guide
- mel365.com — Tuscany travel blog with map resources
- SOI55 Lifestyle (soi55lifestyle.com) — Siena mini-guide, planning services

### B. Film-location and set-jetting publishers (the Bond page's natural home)

Set-jetting is a named, growing trend (National Geographic, Expedia Magazine
have both covered Italy film tourism). The pitch is the page's unique claim:
scene-by-scene mapping with honest set-versus-street verdicts.

- bondlocations.com and thejamesbonddossier.com — Bond-specific location
  references; both already document QoS Siena and may welcome a deeper local
  resource
- movie-locations.com — the standing film-location reference
- Italo Blog, Citalia blog, italien.news — Italy film-tourism roundups that
  update periodically

### C. Institutional and community

- Study-abroad programme pages in Siena/Florence (universities list local
  resources for students)
- Tourism-adjacent local businesses (agriturismi, language schools) with
  "useful links" pages — we already link *out* to official sources; some
  reciprocity here is organic, not a scheme
- HARO/Qwoted-style journalist requests: respond as "the editor of a
  fact-checked Siena guide" with a dated figure and a named source — the
  citation link follows the quote

### D. The tools as embeddable answers

When a forum thread or blog comments section asks "how much does a week in
Tuscany cost?", a personal, helpful answer that *mentions* the calculator is
legitimate participation; a pasted link with no substance is spam. If you
would not post the answer without the link, do not post it.

## 3. Outreach template (adapt, never blast)

> Subject: a dated source for your [topic] page
>
> Hi [name] — I run Archi Travel Guide, an independent Siena guide written
> from here. Your [specific post] covers [topic]; one thing readers often
> miss is [specific fact], which we document with the date we last verified
> it against [official source]: [URL]. If it's useful for your page, great —
> and if you spot anything we got wrong, I genuinely want to know.

Rules: one email, one specific page of theirs, one specific asset of ours, no
follow-up chase beyond a single polite nudge, and never an offer of payment
or reciprocal links.

## 4. Tracking

Keep a simple sheet (site, page pitched, asset offered, date, outcome).
Watch results in Search Console → Links → Top linking sites; it lags weeks.
Expect single digits per month — earned links are slow, and the compounding
is the point.
